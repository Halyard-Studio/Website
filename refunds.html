Refunds and guarantee

Last updated 11 November 2026

If the script doesn't do what we agreed, we fix it free or refund your deposit.

Commissions

  • Before delivery: if we can't deliver what the quote describes, we refund your 50% deposit in full.
  • After delivery: for 14 days we fix anything that doesn't work as agreed, free of charge. If we can't make it work as agreed, we refund your deposit.
  • Changes of plan: if you cancel after work has started, tell us straight away. We refund the part of the deposit that covers work not yet done.
  • Requests for new features or changes beyond the quote are not covered by the guarantee; we quote them separately.

Free releases

Free scripts cost nothing, so there is nothing to refund. We still fix reported bugs and post every fix in our Discord #changelog.

Store products

Paid products are sold through Tebex, which handles payment. If a product doesn't work as described on a supported framework and we can't fix it, contact us within 14 days of purchase for a refund.

How to ask

Email hello@halyard.gg or open a ticket in our Discord with your invoice or order number and what's wrong. A person reads every request and replies within a few hours.
